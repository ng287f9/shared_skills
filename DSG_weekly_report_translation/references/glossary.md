## Style notes
- Register: internal engineering/program-management report — concise, neutral, technical. Avoid marketing or overly casual phrasing.
- Keep Simplified Chinese (zh-CN) throughout.
- When an English sentence embeds an acronym (e.g. "Honeywell RL5.2.1 software download"), keep the acronym/version number untranslated and in Latin characters even mid-sentence in the Chinese line, exactly as the reference deck does (e.g. "霍尼韦尔RL5.2.1软件的下载").
- "Honeywell" is sometimes shortened to "霍尼" and sometimes written in full as "霍尼韦尔" in the reference deck — prefer the full "霍尼韦尔" on first mention in a section and "霍尼" is acceptable in shorter table-cell contexts where space is tight, matching the reference deck's own inconsistency. If the user asks to standardize, pick one and use it everywhere.
- Section labels that act as headings stay English-only (Bucket A): slide 4's `SN1002:` / `SN1003:` / `SN1004:` / `Status:` and the slide-3 footer "Program risks are outlined in program risk file…" are left untranslated — this matches the approved template and all prior week outputs (CW28–CW30).
- Slide-3 section headers "Highlights 亮点" / "Lowlights 不足" DO get an inline CN run (matching the template) even though some prior weeks (CW28, CW30) omitted it — follow the template.
- Callout boxes: Rectangle 7 gets inline CN (Bucket B, sz=1000) per paragraph; Rectangle 4 gets its CN as a separate centered paragraph (sz=1000, muted `5F6F82`) under the EN lines.
